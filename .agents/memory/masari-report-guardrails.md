name: Masari report guardrails
description: User-stated product constraints for assessment interpretation and career recommendations.
---

Reports are for self-discovery, not medical or psychological diagnosis. Do not invent scientific-looking scores or guarantee career success. Tie recommendations to the person's answers and include practical trades, crafts, and technical work when relevant. The first version requires no account or phone number. Explain that local JSON reports may not survive some restarts or redeployments.

**Why:** These are explicit requirements from the product brief.

**How to apply:** Preserve these constraints in Grok prompts, report presentation, privacy behavior, and future storage changes.
